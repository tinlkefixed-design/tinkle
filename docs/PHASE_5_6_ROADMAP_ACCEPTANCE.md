# TINKLE Roadmap Stages 5–6 Acceptance

## Stage 5 — Invention Generation Engine
- Structured invention/design schemas and Design Genome.
- Deterministic architecture × mechanism × material × process × algorithm search.
- Candidate generation is real, bounded, deduplicated and resource-capped.
- Novelty classification is explicitly separated from patentability and scientific validity.
- Every candidate carries provenance, assumptions and evidence references.

## Stage 6 — Cross-Domain Innovation Engine
- Domain observations are converted into abstract transferable principles.
- Cross-domain pairs are searched without requiring a single fixed domain.
- Mechanism mapping and verification requirements are explicit.
- Evidence references are preserved; analogy never upgrades evidence.
- Candidates remain `CANDIDATE` until mathematical, constraint, simulation and/or experimental validation.

## Definition of Done
Implementation, API integration, unit tests, regression compatibility, documentation and compile verification are required. No mock is used in the production path.

## Explicit boundary
These stages do not claim autonomous scientific invention, world-level novelty, patentability, or validated performance. They create auditable candidate design spaces and cross-domain hypotheses for later simulation/falsification/validation stages.
